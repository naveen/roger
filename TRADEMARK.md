# The Roger name

> Draft. This is a statement of intent written alongside the first version of the repository. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

The code, hardware and design files in this repository are open under the licences listed in the README. The name **Roger**, as used for this product, and the Roger logo (the orange dot and wordmark) are not part of what those licences grant.

The reason is the user. A parent who sees "Roger" on a radio should be able to assume that what their child hears has been through the Roger project's safety work. A modified relay with a different prompt may be better or worse, and it is not the same thing.

## What you can do without asking

- Build radios and relays from this repository for yourself, your family and your friends, and call them Roger.
- Say that your project is "built on Roger", "compatible with Roger" or "a fork of Roger".
- Use the name and logo to refer to this project in articles, talks and reviews.

## What needs a different name

- Selling or distributing a device, kit or hosted service, with the Roger name or logo on it.
- Offering a relay to other families under the Roger name with a changed persona prompt, guard or rules.

If you fork the project to make your own product, pick your own name and logo. Everything else is yours to use under the licences.
