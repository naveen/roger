## How it works

**Talking to AI already sounds like a walkie-talkie. So we made it one.**

1. **Hold to talk.** One big button. Roger only listens while it's pressed — no wake word, no always-on microphone.
2. **Let go. Kshhh…** A little radio static while Roger thinks. The pause isn't a bug — it's what makes it feel like a real radio.
3. **"Roger that!"** A short, spoken answer pitched at your kid's age — usually ending with a reason to go look, dig or build.

## Two promises

**Built for outside.** Rain, puddles, sandboxes, the bottom of a backpack. Rubber bumper, sealed speaker, a clip and a strap loop. Charge it at night, carry it all day.

**Nothing to stare at.** No screen, no feed, no apps. Just a voice, a button and a volume knob — so eyes stay on the worm, not the device.

## For grown-ups

**A model made for kids, not adapted for them.**

Behind the button is a clean, kid-safe AI model. It answers at your child's level, steers around what isn't theirs to carry yet, and points them back to you when a question needs a parent.

- **Listens only when pressed.** The microphone is physically off until the button is held.
- **A daily digest, not a spy feed.** The parent app shows what your kid was curious about today, plus a heads-up if something needs you.
- **Your rules.** Quiet hours, daily air-time, volume cap, and topics to steer around.
- **No ads. Ever.** Nothing is sold to your kid, and their voice isn't used to sell anything.

## Questions

**What ages is Roger for?**
Roughly 4 to 12. You set the age during setup and Roger adjusts its words and topics.

**Does it need Wi-Fi?**
It needs a connection to answer. We're still working out the right mix of Wi-Fi and cellular for backyard-and-beyond range.

**Can kids talk to each other?**
Not at launch. Roger starts as kid-to-AI only. Parent-approved friend channels are something we're exploring — tell us on the waitlist if you'd want them.

## Where the promises stand today

| The page says | In this repository |
|---|---|
| "Roger only listens while it's pressed" | True in firmware, and checked by the relay. |
| "The microphone is physically off until the button is held" | Not yet. Software-gated on both boards. An untested wiring option exists for the DIY build. |
| "A kind, kid-safe AI" / "A model made for kids" | A general model with a kids' persona prompt and a guard. Not independently evaluated. |
| "Rubber bumper, sealed speaker" | An optional printed TPU band. Nothing is sealed. |
| "Charge it at night, carry it all day" | No power management yet. Battery life is unmeasured and short. |
| "The parent app" | A daily email and a JSON file. The app is wireframes. |
| "Quiet hours, daily air-time, volume cap, topics to steer around" | All four work in the relay. |
| "No ads. Ever." | True. There is nowhere for one to go. |
