Built for outside.

Rain, puddles, sandboxes, the bottom of a backpack. Rubber bumper, sealed speaker, a clip and a strap loop. Charge it at night, carry it all day.

Nothing to stare at.

No screen, no feed, no apps. Just a voice, a button and a volume knob — so eyes stay on the worm, not the device.

For grown-ups

A model made for kids, not adapted for them.

Behind the button is a clean, kid-safe AI model. It answers at your child’s level, steers around what isn’t theirs to carry yet, and points them back to you when a question needs a parent.

Listens only when pressed

The microphone is physically off until the button is held.

A daily digest, not a spy feed

The parent app shows what your kid was curious about today, plus a heads-up if something needs you.

Your rules

Quiet hours, daily air-time, volume cap, and topics to steer around.

No ads. Ever.

Nothing is sold to your kid, and their voice isn’t used to sell anything.

Questions

What ages is Roger for?

Roughly 4 to 12. You set the age during setup and Roger adjusts its words and topics.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

It needs a connection to answer. We’re still working out the right mix of Wi-Fi and cellular for backyard-and-beyond range.

Can kids talk to each other?

Not at launch. Roger starts as kid-to-AI only. Parent-approved friend channels are something we’re exploring — tell us on the waitlist if you’d want them.
